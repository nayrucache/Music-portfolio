A bit about me.

[ BIO ]

My name is Samuel Watsham and I'm an independent composer / producer and NYU Music Technology undergraduate. My artistic journey solidified itself at Interlochen Arts Academy where I was studying classical guitar performance and simultaneously found my love for all things music production, technology, and engineering. After my senior year I moved to NYC to attend NYU as a Music Tech major. I've been based here for three years now and in that time have been a part of many inspiring and innovative projects that continue to redefine my perception of the creative world. All tracks are original works.
